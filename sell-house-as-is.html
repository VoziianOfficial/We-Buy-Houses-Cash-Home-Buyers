Describe what you know

Useful property details for an as-is review.

Accurate information helps the team understand the property before requesting clarification, access, or additional documentation.

Structure

Mention known foundation concerns, roof damage, siding issues, drainage problems, broken windows, and exterior deterioration.

Systems

Share the known condition of heating, cooling, electrical, plumbing, water, sewer, septic, and other major systems.

Interior

Describe flooring, walls, ceilings, kitchens, bathrooms, fixtures, odors, damage, and rooms that are unfinished or difficult to access.

Access

Explain whether the home is owner occupied, vacant, inherited, tenant occupied, secured, or subject to limited access.

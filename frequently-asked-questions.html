Helpful submission details

Tell the team what may affect the property review.

You do not need to know every technical detail. Share the information available to you and explain any uncertainty in the message field.

  • Include the complete street address, city, state, and ZIP code.
  • Describe known repairs, visible damage, unfinished work, and deferred maintenance.
  • Explain whether the property is occupied, rented, vacant, or inherited.
  • Mention multiple owners, title concerns, probate, liens, or other known ownership circumstances.
  • Share your preferred timeline even when the schedule remains flexible.

Policy overview

About this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to information collected through the Velmora Home Offers website and related property-request communications operated by Velmora Property Solutions LLC.

By using this website or submitting information through the property form, you acknowledge that the information may be processed as described in this policy.

Submitting property information does not guarantee an offer and does not create an obligation to sell. Information is collected so the request can be reviewed and answered.

Information categories

Information We Collect

We may collect information that you provide directly, information generated when you use the website, and information needed to review a property-related request.

Information submitted through the property form

  • Your full name and email address.
  • The property address, city, state, and ZIP code.
  • The property type, current condition, and occupancy status.
  • Your preferred selling timeline.
  • An optional expected price or other pricing context.
  • Additional information included in the message field, such as repairs, access limitations, occupants, title circumstances, remaining belongings, relocation needs, or other property details.
  • The page from which the request was submitted.
  • Your confirmation that you reviewed the Privacy Policy and consented to the use of the submitted information.

Website and technical information

Depending on the website hosting, security, and analytics tools in use, technical information may be processed automatically. This may include:

  • Internet Protocol address and general device information.
  • Browser type, operating system, and approximate location derived from technical data.
  • Pages visited, referring pages, visit times, and interaction information.
  • Security logs, error reports, and information used to detect suspicious or automated submissions.
  • The cookie or storage preference selected through the website consent notice.

Information not requested

The property form does not request a telephone number. Please do not submit highly sensitive personal information, account credentials, government identification numbers, payment card information, medical records, or unrelated confidential information through the website.

Processing purposes

How We Use Information

Information collected through this website may be used for the following purposes:

  • To receive, organize, and review property submissions.
  • To understand the property location, type, condition, occupancy, access, ownership circumstances, and seller timeline.
  • To contact you by email when clarification or additional property information is needed.
  • To explain whether an available next step or potential written offer may be considered.
  • To coordinate transaction-related communication when the relevant parties decide to continue.
  • To maintain internal records relating to requests, communications, and property reviews.
  • To operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the website.
  • To detect spam, automated submissions, fraud, misuse, security threats, or unlawful activity.
  • To comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce website terms.

The email address submitted through the form is used to review and respond to the request. The website does not require a telephone number.

Limited disclosure

How Information May Be Shared

Information may be disclosed when reasonably necessary to operate the website, review a property request, communicate with you, or support a potential transaction.

Service providers

Information may be processed by vendors that provide website hosting, email delivery, form handling, security, technical support, analytics, document management, or other operational services.

Transaction professionals

When a request progresses beyond an initial review, relevant information may be shared with professionals involved in evaluating or coordinating the transaction, such as title companies, attorneys, closing providers, inspectors, contractors, financing partners, property specialists, or other authorized participants.

Legal and safety requirements

Information may be disclosed when reasonably believed necessary to comply with applicable law, legal process, regulatory requirements, enforceable requests, protect rights or property, investigate fraud, or address safety and security concerns.

Business changes

Information may be transferred as part of a merger, reorganization, financing, sale of assets, acquisition, or other business transaction involving Velmora Property Solutions LLC.

We do not intend to sell property-form information to unrelated companies for their independent consumer marketing.

Browser technology

Cookies and Local Storage

The website may use cookies, browser storage, or similar technologies to support essential functionality, remember preferences, improve security, and understand website performance.

Essential storage

The website stores the cookie preference selected through the consent notice. This allows the website to remember whether you selected “Accept” or “Essential Only.”

Optional technologies

Additional analytics or measurement technologies should only be used in accordance with the selected preference and the tools actually installed on the website.

You can remove stored preferences through your browser settings. Clearing browser data may cause the cookie notice to appear again.

More information is available in the Cookie Policy .

Data management

Retention and Security

Information retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to review and respond to a request, maintain business records, support a potential transaction, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent misuse, and protect legitimate business interests.

Retention periods may vary depending on the nature of the information, whether a transaction progresses, applicable legal requirements, and operational needs.

Security measures

Reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures may be used to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, loss, misuse, or disclosure.

No website, email system, storage service, or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Submit only the information reasonably needed for the property request.

Access and preferences

To submit a privacy-related request, send an email to:

Additional protections

Children and External Websites

Children’s privacy

This website is intended for adults seeking information about a residential property or potential business collaboration. It is not directed to children, and information from children is not knowingly requested through the property form.

A parent or guardian who believes a child submitted personal information may contact Velmora Property Solutions LLC to request review and appropriate action.

External links

The website may contain links to third-party websites, services, or resources. Their privacy practices are controlled by their own policies. Velmora Property Solutions LLC is not responsible for the content, security, or privacy practices of unrelated external websites.

Policy revisions

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes in website functionality, information practices, operational requirements, service providers, legal obligations, or business processes.

The revised policy will be published on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the website after a revision means the updated policy will apply to future use and future submissions.

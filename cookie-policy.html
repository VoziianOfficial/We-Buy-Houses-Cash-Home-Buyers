Policy overview

About This Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy applies to browser storage and similar technologies used through the Velmora Home Offers website operated by Velmora Property Solutions LLC.

The website may use these technologies to support core functionality, remember visitor preferences, improve security, detect technical problems, and understand website performance.

This policy should be read together with the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service .

The current website stores your cookie preference in browser local storage so the consent notice does not need to appear on every visit.

Browser technologies

What Cookies and Similar Technologies Are

Cookies are small text files that a website or service provider may place on a visitor’s device through a web browser. They can help a website remember settings, maintain functionality, understand usage, and support security.

Similar technologies may include:

  • Browser local storage.
  • Session storage.
  • Tracking pixels or tags.
  • Embedded scripts.
  • Device or browser identifiers.
  • Server logs and related technical records.

Some technologies remain available only during a browser session. Others may remain stored until they expire or are removed through browser controls.

Core website functionality

Essential Browser Storage

Essential technologies support functions that are reasonably necessary for the website to operate, remember a visitor’s privacy selection, protect forms, or maintain website security.

Cookie preference storage

The website uses browser local storage to remember whether a visitor selected:

  • “Accept.”
  • “Essential Only.”

The preference is stored under a browser storage key configured for the Velmora Home Offers website. It is used only to remember the selected consent state.

Form and security support

Hosting, email, form-processing, or security providers may use essential technical records to:

  • Deliver website pages and files.
  • Process property-form submissions.
  • Detect spam or automated activity.
  • Prevent abuse and security threats.
  • Diagnose website errors.
  • Maintain service availability.

Essential storage may remain active when “Essential Only” is selected because the website needs to remember that preference and support core functionality.

Non-essential tools

Optional Technologies

Optional technologies may be added to understand website performance, measure visitor interaction, improve content, or evaluate marketing effectiveness.

Depending on the tools actually installed, optional categories may include:

Analytics

Analytics tools may collect information about page visits, navigation, browser type, device type, approximate location, referring pages, visit duration, and website interactions.

Performance measurement

Performance tools may help identify slow pages, script errors, failed resources, layout problems, and other technical issues.

Advertising or campaign measurement

Advertising or campaign tools may measure whether a visitor arrived through a particular advertisement, search result, referral, or promotional source.

Optional analytics or advertising technologies should not be treated as active unless they have actually been installed and configured on the published website.

Consent options

Your Cookie Preferences

When the cookie notice appears, you may choose between the available preference options.

Accept

Selecting “Accept” records an accepted preference. When optional technologies are installed and properly connected to the consent system, this choice may allow those technologies to operate.

Essential Only

Selecting “Essential Only” records a preference that limits the website to technologies reasonably necessary for core operation, security, form handling, and preference storage.

Changing your selection

You can remove the stored choice through your browser’s site-data controls. After the stored value is removed, the cookie notice should appear again during a future visit.

Browser settings differ by browser and device. Look for controls related to cookies, local storage, site data, privacy, or website permissions.

External providers

Third-Party Technologies

The website may rely on third-party providers for hosting, email delivery, form processing, security, analytics, fonts, media, content delivery, or technical functionality.

Depending on the provider and configuration, these services may process technical information or place browser technologies on the visitor’s device.

Third-party technologies may be used for:

  • Website hosting and content delivery.
  • Form submission and email delivery.
  • Spam and security protection.
  • Website analytics and performance monitoring.
  • Error reporting and troubleshooting.
  • Embedded media or external resources.

Third-party providers operate under their own privacy and cookie practices. Velmora Property Solutions LLC does not control every technology used by an external hosting or infrastructure provider.

External services should be reviewed before publication to ensure that this policy accurately reflects the technologies installed on the live website.

Retention period

How Long Browser Data May Remain

The duration of browser storage depends on the technology, its purpose, the provider’s configuration, and whether the visitor removes the stored data.

Local preference storage

The website’s cookie-preference value may remain in local storage until it is removed through browser controls, replaced by a new selection, or cleared by the browser or device.

Session technologies

Session-based technologies generally remain active only while the browser session is open, although exact behavior may vary by browser and provider.

Provider-controlled technologies

Technologies operated by hosting, security, analytics, or other third-party providers may use their own expiration and retention periods.

Device settings

Managing Cookies and Site Data

Most browsers allow visitors to inspect, block, delete, or limit cookies and browser storage.

Browser controls may allow you to:

  • Delete cookies and local storage for this website.
  • Block third-party cookies.
  • Block all cookies.
  • Clear browsing data when the browser closes.
  • Review individual website permissions.
  • Limit tracking or advertising identifiers.

Blocking all browser storage may affect preference memory, form protection, security tools, or other website functions.

Do Not Track and browser privacy signals

Browser privacy signals are not implemented uniformly across all websites and services. The website’s response may depend on the technologies actually installed and the capabilities of their providers.
